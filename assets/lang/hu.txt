Refreshing by itself	Önálló frissítés
The weather, the warnings and the earthquakes come by themselves, also with the app closed (for the widgets): every 15 minutes while the screen is on, every 30 minutes with the screen off, not at all between sunset and sunrise while the screen is off (it waits for the screen to come on), and every hour when the battery is below 25% and not charging. When a download fails, a notice and the line "! no data since …" under the clock say why (no internet, the server did not answer, secure connection error, server error…). When Open-Meteo is busy (HTTP 503 "overloaded") or does not answer, the forecast comes from MET Norway (api.met.no, the Norwegian weather institute) and the line under the clock says "· MET Norway"; after a "busy" the app goes straight to MET Norway for 20 minutes. Until new data comes, the last saved weather stays on the screen. Without internet the app does not try again and again: it waits for the network to come back, then brings the weather at once.	Az időjárás, a figyelmeztetések és a földrengések maguktól jönnek, zárt alkalmazásnál is (a widgetekhez): 15 percenként bekapcsolt kijelzőnél, 30 percenként kikapcsoltnál, egyáltalán nem napnyugta és napkelte között kikapcsolt kijelzőnél (megvárja, amíg bekapcsol), és óránként, ha az akkumulátor 25% alatt van és nem töltődik.
