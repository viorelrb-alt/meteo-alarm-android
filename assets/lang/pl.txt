There are three layouts: 2x2 (square: icon, temperature, town, next sunset or sunrise), 4x1 (a low strip, also with the sky) and 4x2 (wide, also with high / low and the next 5 hours), plus a small 2x1 (icon and temperature) for frames too small for them. Hold it and drag its edges: wide (about 4 columns) and one row = 4x1, wide and taller = 4x2 (the next hours, or the next days for the 4x2 · days; 3 rows or more = 4x3, with both), narrow = 2x2, narrow and one row = 2x1. The rows and columns are those of your home screen (on a tablet a row is much taller than on a phone), so the 4x1 strip works there too. It fills the frame's width; when the frame is taller than the layout needs, the widget keeps its size, centred, with the background only behind it (the rest stays see-through). A layout is never shrunk so much that the texts get too small. With active warnings a row "WARNING!" shows three pills: yellow = gray gray yellow, orange = gray orange orange, red = all red; two codes = the worse one, gray, the other; three codes = red orange yellow. Refreshing by itself	Samoczynne odświeżanie
The weather, the warnings and the earthquakes come by themselves, also with the app closed (for the widgets): every 15 minutes while the screen is on, every 30 minutes with the screen off, not at all between sunset and sunrise while the screen is off (it waits for the screen to come on), and every hour when the battery is below 25% and not charging. When a download fails, a notice and the line "! no data since …" under the clock say why (no internet, the server did not answer, secure connection error, server error…). When Open-Meteo is busy (HTTP 503 "overloaded") or does not answer, the forecast comes from MET Norway (api.met.no, the Norwegian weather institute) and the line under the clock says "· MET Norway"; after a "busy" the app goes straight to MET Norway for 20 minutes. Until new data comes, the last saved weather stays on the screen. Without internet the app does not try again and again: it waits for the network to come back, then brings the weather at once.	Pogoda, ostrzeżenia i trzęsienia ziemi przychodzą same, także przy zamkniętej aplikacji (dla widżetów): co 15 minut przy włączonym ekranie, co 30 minut przy wyłączonym, wcale między zachodem a wschodem słońca przy wyłączonym ekranie (czeka na włączenie ekranu) i co godzinę, gdy bateria ma poniżej 25% i się nie ładuje. Gdy pobieranie się nie uda, komunikat i wiersz „! brak danych od …” pod zegarem mówią dlaczego (brak internetu, serwer nie odpowiedział, błąd bezpiecznego połączenia, błąd serwera…).
