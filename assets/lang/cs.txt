No data collected: no account, no ads, no statistics. To get the weather, only the place's coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo (weather, pollen) and, when Open-Meteo is overloaded, to MET Norway (weather); the warnings come from MeteoAlarm, the earthquakes from EMSC (seismicportal.eu). Meteo Alarm widgets	Widgety Meteo Alarm
Hold a free spot on the home screen, choose Widgets, then Meteo Alarm: 2x2, 4x1, 4x2, 4x2 · days and "any size", each with its preview; it goes on the screen at once, without a settings window. There are three layouts: 2x2 (square: icon, temperature, town, next sunset or sunrise), 4x1 (a low strip, also with the sky) and 4x2 (wide, also with high / low and the next 5 hours), plus a small 2x1 (icon and temperature) for frames too small for them. Hold it and drag its edges: wide (about 4 columns) and one row = 4x1, wide and taller = 4x2 (the next hours, or the next days for the 4x2 · days; 3 rows or more = 4x3, with both), narrow = 2x2, narrow and one row = 2x1. The rows and columns are those of your home screen (on a tablet a row is much taller than on a phone), so the 4x1 strip works there too. It fills the frame's width; when the frame is taller than the layout needs, the widget keeps its size, centred, with the background only behind it (the rest stays see-through). A layout is never shrunk so much that the texts get too small. With active warnings a row "WARNING!" shows three pills: yellow = gray gray yellow, orange = gray orange orange, red = all red; two codes = the worse one, gray, the other; three codes = red orange yellow. Refreshing by itself	Samočinné obnovování
The weather, the warnings and the earthquakes come by themselves, also with the app closed (for the widgets): every 15 minutes while the screen is on, every 30 minutes with the screen off, not at all between sunset and sunrise while the screen is off (it waits for the screen to come on), and every hour when the battery is below 25% and not charging. When a download fails, a notice and the line "! no data since …" under the clock say why (no internet, the server did not answer, secure connection error, server error…). When Open-Meteo is busy (HTTP 503 "overloaded") or does not answer, the forecast comes from MET Norway (api.met.no, the Norwegian weather institute) and the line under the clock says "· MET Norway"; after a "busy" the app goes straight to MET Norway for 20 minutes. Until new data comes, the last saved weather stays on the screen. Without internet the app does not try again and again: it waits for the network to come back, then brings the weather at once.	Počasí, výstrahy a zemětřesení přicházejí samy, i při zavřené aplikaci (pro widgety): každých 15 minut se zapnutým displejem, každých 30 minut s vypnutým, vůbec ne mezi západem a východem slunce s vypnutým displejem (čeká se na zapnutí displeje) a každou hodinu, když je baterie pod 25 % a nenabíjí se. Když stahování selže, upozornění a řádek „! žádná data od …“ pod hodinami řeknou proč (bez internetu, server neodpověděl, chyba zabezpečeného spojení, chyba serveru…). Když je Open-Meteo vytížené (HTTP 503 „overloaded“) nebo neodpovídá, předpověď přijde z MET Norway (api.met.no, norský meteorologický ústav) a pod hodinami stojí „· MET Norway“; po „vytížené“ jde aplikace 20 minut rovnou na MET Norway. Dokud nepřijdou nová data, zůstává na obrazovce naposledy uložené počasí. Bez internetu to aplikace nezkouší znovu a znovu: počká, až se síť vrátí, a hned stáhne počasí.
