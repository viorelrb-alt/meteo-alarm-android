Meteo Alarm widgets	Pripomočki Meteo Alarm
Hold a free spot on the home screen, choose Widgets, then Meteo Alarm: 2x2, 4x1, 4x2, 4x2 · days and "any size", each with its preview; it goes on the screen at once, without a settings window. There are three layouts: 2x2 (square: icon, temperature, town, next sunset or sunrise), 4x1 (a low strip, also with the sky) and 4x2 (wide, also with high / low and the next 5 hours), plus a small 2x1 (icon and temperature) for frames too small for them. Hold it and drag its edges: wide (about 4 columns) and one row = 4x1, wide and taller = 4x2 (the next hours, or the next days for the 4x2 · days; 3 rows or more = 4x3, with both), narrow = 2x2, narrow and one row = 2x1. The rows and columns are those of your home screen (on a tablet a row is much taller than on a phone), so the 4x1 strip works there too. It fills the frame's width; when the frame is taller than the layout needs, the widget keeps its size, centred, with the background only behind it (the rest stays see-through). A layout is never shrunk so much that the texts get too small. With active warnings a row "WARNING!" shows three pills: yellow = gray gray yellow, orange = gray orange orange, red = all red; two codes = the worse one, gray, the other; three codes = red orange yellow. A tap opens the app.	Pridržite prazno mesto na začetnem zaslonu, izberite Pripomočki, nato Meteo Alarm: 2x2, 4x1, 4x2, 4x2 · dnevi in »poljubna velikost«, vsak s predogledom; gre takoj na zaslon, brez okna z nastavitvami. Refreshing by itself	Samodejno osveževanje
The weather, the warnings and the earthquakes come by themselves, also with the app closed (for the widgets): every 15 minutes while the screen is on, every 30 minutes with the screen off, not at all between sunset and sunrise while the screen is off (it waits for the screen to come on), and every hour when the battery is below 25% and not charging. When a download fails, a notice and the line "! no data since …" under the clock say why (no internet, the server did not answer, secure connection error, server error…). When Open-Meteo is busy (HTTP 503 "overloaded") or does not answer, the forecast comes from MET Norway (api.met.no, the Norwegian weather institute) and the line under the clock says "· MET Norway"; after a "busy" the app goes straight to MET Norway for 20 minutes. Until new data comes, the last saved weather stays on the screen. Without internet the app does not try again and again: it waits for the network to come back, then brings the weather at once.	Vreme, opozorila in potresi pridejo sami, tudi pri zaprti aplikaciji (za pripomočke): vsakih 15 minut s prižganim zaslonom, vsakih 30 minut z ugasnjenim, sploh ne med sončnim zahodom in vzhodom z ugasnjenim zaslonom (počaka, da se zaslon prižge), in vsako uro, ko je baterija pod 25 % in se ne polni. Ko prenos ne uspe, obvestilo in vrstica »! ni podatkov od …« pod uro povesta, zakaj (ni interneta, strežnik ni odgovoril, napaka varne povezave, napaka strežnika…). Ko je Open-Meteo zaseden (HTTP 503 »overloaded«) ali ne odgovarja, napoved pride z MET Norway (api.met.no, norveški meteorološki inštitut) in pod uro piše »· MET Norway«; po »zaseden« gre aplikacija 20 minut naravnost na MET Norway. Dokler ne pridejo novi podatki, ostane na zaslonu zadnje shranjeno vreme. Brez interneta aplikacija ne poskuša znova in znova: počaka, da se omrežje vrne, nato takoj prinese vreme.
