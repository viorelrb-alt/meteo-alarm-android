Meteo Alarm widgets	Miniaplikácie Meteo Alarm
Hold a free spot on the home screen, choose Widgets, then Meteo Alarm: 2x2, 4x1, 4x2 and "any size", each with its preview; it goes on the screen at once, without a settings window. There are three layouts: 2x2 (square: icon, temperature, town, next sunset or sunrise), 4x1 (a low strip, also with the sky) and 4x2 (wide, also with high / low and the next 5 hours), plus a small 2x1 (icon and temperature) for frames too small for them. Hold it and drag its edges: wide (about 4 columns) and one row = 4x1, wide and taller = 4x2 (it stays 4x2 however tall you make it), narrow = 2x2, narrow and one row = 2x1. The rows and columns are those of your home screen (on a tablet a row is much taller than on a phone), so the 4x1 strip works there too. It fills the frame's width; when the frame is taller than the layout needs, the widget keeps its size, centred, with the background only behind it (the rest stays see-through). A layout is never shrunk so much that the texts get too small. With active warnings a row "WARNING!" shows three pills: yellow = gray gray yellow, orange = gray orange orange, red = all red; two codes = the worse one, gray, the other; three codes = red orange yellow. A tap opens the app.	Podržte prázdne miesto na domovskej obrazovke, zvoľte Miniaplikácie, potom Meteo Alarm: 2x2, 4x1, 4x2 a „ľubovoľná veľkosť“, každá s náhľadom; ide hneď na obrazovku, bez okna nastavení. Sú tri rozloženia: 2x2 (štvorec: ikona, teplota, obec, ďalší západ alebo východ slnka), 4x1 (nízky pás, aj s oblohou) a 4x2 (široký, aj s max. / min. a ďalšími 5 hodinami), plus malý 2x1 (ikona a teplota) pre príliš malé rámy. Podržte ju a ťahajte za okraje: široká (asi 4 stĺpce) a jeden riadok = 4x1, široká a vyššia = 4x2 (zostane 4x2, nech je akokoľvek vysoká), úzka = 2x2, úzka a jeden riadok = 2x1. Refreshing by itself	Samočinné obnovovanie
The weather, the warnings and the earthquakes come by themselves, also with the app closed (for the widgets): every 15 minutes while the screen is on, every 30 minutes with the screen off, not at all between sunset and sunrise while the screen is off (it waits for the screen to come on), and every hour when the battery is below 25% and not charging. When a download fails, a notice and the line "! no data since …" under the clock say why (no internet, the server did not answer, secure connection error, server error…). When Open-Meteo is busy (HTTP 503 "overloaded") or does not answer, the forecast comes from MET Norway (api.met.no, the Norwegian weather institute) and the line under the clock says "· MET Norway"; after a "busy" the app goes straight to MET Norway for 20 minutes. Until new data comes, the last saved weather stays on the screen.	Počasie, výstrahy a zemetrasenia prichádzajú samy, aj pri zatvorenej aplikácii (pre miniaplikácie): každých 15 minút so zapnutou obrazovkou, každých 30 minút s vypnutou, vôbec nie medzi západom a východom slnka s vypnutou obrazovkou (čaká sa na jej zapnutie) a každú hodinu, keď je batéria pod 25 % a nenabíja sa. Keď sťahovanie zlyhá, upozornenie a riadok „! žiadne údaje od …“ pod hodinami povedia prečo (bez internetu, server neodpovedal, chyba zabezpečeného spojenia, chyba servera…). Keď je Open-Meteo vyťažené (HTTP 503 „overloaded“) alebo neodpovedá, predpoveď príde z MET Norway (api.met.no, nórsky meteorologický ústav) a pod hodinami stojí „· MET Norway“; po „vyťažené“ ide aplikácia 20 minút rovno na MET Norway. Kým neprídu nové údaje, zostáva na obrazovke naposledy uložené počasie.
