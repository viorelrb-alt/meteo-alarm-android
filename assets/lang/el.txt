Meteo Alarm widgets	Γραφικά στοιχεία Meteo Alarm
Hold a free spot on the home screen, choose Widgets, then Meteo Alarm: 2x2, 4x1, 4x2, 4x2 · days and "any size", each with its preview; it goes on the screen at once, without a settings window. There are three layouts: 2x2 (square: icon, temperature, town, next sunset or sunrise), 4x1 (a low strip, also with the sky) and 4x2 (wide, also with high / low and the next 5 hours), plus a small 2x1 (icon and temperature) for frames too small for them. Hold it and drag its edges: wide (about 4 columns) and one row = 4x1, wide and taller = 4x2 (the next hours, or the next days for the 4x2 · days; 3 rows or more = 4x3, with both), narrow = 2x2, narrow and one row = 2x1. The rows and columns are those of your home screen (on a tablet a row is much taller than on a phone), so the 4x1 strip works there too. It fills the frame's width; when the frame is taller than the layout needs, the widget keeps its size, centred, with the background only behind it (the rest stays see-through). Refreshing by itself	Αυτόματη ανανέωση
The weather, the warnings and the earthquakes come by themselves, also with the app closed (for the widgets): every 15 minutes while the screen is on, every 30 minutes with the screen off, not at all between sunset and sunrise while the screen is off (it waits for the screen to come on), and every hour when the battery is below 25% and not charging. When a download fails, a notice and the line "! no data since …" under the clock say why (no internet, the server did not answer, secure connection error, server error…). When Open-Meteo is busy (HTTP 503 "overloaded") or does not answer, the forecast comes from MET Norway (api.met.no, the Norwegian weather institute) and the line under the clock says "· MET Norway"; after a "busy" the app goes straight to MET Norway for 20 minutes. Until new data comes, the last saved weather stays on the screen.	Ο καιρός, οι προειδοποιήσεις και οι σεισμοί έρχονται μόνοι τους, και με κλειστή εφαρμογή (για τα γραφικά στοιχεία): κάθε 15 λεπτά με αναμμένη οθόνη, κάθε 30 λεπτά με σβηστή, καθόλου μεταξύ δύσης και ανατολής με σβηστή οθόνη (περιμένει να ανάψει) και κάθε ώρα όταν η μπαταρία είναι κάτω από 25% και δεν φορτίζει. In Help, Earthquakes, About and What's new: the bar on the right, under the close button. Slide on it up for larger letters, down for smaller ones; the size is kept on the device. Σύρετε πάνω για μεγαλύτερα γράμματα, κάτω για μικρότερα· το μέγεθος αποθηκεύεται στη συσκευή. Σε tablet το μενού ☰ είναι 30 % μεγαλύτερο.
