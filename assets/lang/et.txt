This is a test version of Meteo Alarm Android, made from Meteo Alarm Kindle.	See on Meteo Alarm Androidi testversioon, tehtud Meteo Alarm Kindle'i põhjal.
No data collected: no account, no ads, no statistics. To get the weather, only the place's coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo (weather, pollen) and, when Open-Meteo is overloaded, to MET Norway (weather); the warnings come from MeteoAlarm, the earthquakes from EMSC (seismicportal.eu).	Andmeid ei koguta: pole kontot, reklaame ega statistikat. Ilma saamiseks saadetakse ainult koha koordinaadid Open-Meteole (ilm, õietolm) ja, kui Open-Meteo on ülekoormatud, MET Norwayle (ilm); hoiatused tulevad MeteoAlarmilt, maavärinad EMSC-lt (seismicportal.eu).
Location: used to show the weather where you are, at start and once an hour. To name the town, the coordinates go to OpenStreetMap (or the phone's geocoder), which also gives the town's boundary. Towns found, with where you were, are kept only on the phone. The app asks for it with an explanation at the first start; if you say no, you choose the town from the list and it asks again only after an update.	Asukoht: kasutatakse ilma näitamiseks seal, kus sa oled, käivitamisel ja kord tunnis. Linna nimetamiseks lähevad koordinaadid OpenStreetMapile (või telefoni geokodeerijale), mis annab ka linna piiri. Leitud linnu koos sellega, kus sa olid, hoitakse ainult telefonis. Rakendus küsib seda esimesel käivitamisel koos selgitusega; kui keeldud, valid linna loendist ja see küsib uuesti alles pärast uuendust.

Meteo Alarm widgets	Meteo Alarmi vidinad
Hold a free spot on the home screen, choose Widgets, then Meteo Alarm: 2x2, 4x1, 4x2, 4x2 · days and "any size", each with its preview; it goes on the screen at once, without a settings window. There are three layouts: 2x2 (square: icon, temperature, town, next sunset or sunrise), 4x1 (a low strip, also with the sky) and 4x2 (wide, also with high / low and the next 5 hours), plus a small 2x1 (icon and temperature) for frames too small for them. Hold it and drag its edges: wide (about 4 columns) and one row = 4x1, wide and taller = 4x2 (the next hours, or the next days for the 4x2 · days; 3 rows or more = 4x3, with both), narrow = 2x2, narrow and one row = 2x1. The rows and columns are those of your home screen (on a tablet a row is much taller than on a phone), so the 4x1 strip works there too. It fills the frame's width; when the frame is taller than the layout needs, the widget keeps its size, centred, with the background only behind it (the rest stays see-through). A layout is never shrunk so much that the texts get too small. With active warnings a row "WARNING!" shows three pills: yellow = gray gray yellow, orange = gray orange orange, red = all red; two codes = the worse one, gray, the other; three codes = red orange yellow. Refreshing by itself	Värskendab ise
The weather, the warnings and the earthquakes come by themselves, also with the app closed (for the widgets): every 15 minutes while the screen is on, every 30 minutes with the screen off, not at all between sunset and sunrise while the screen is off (it waits for the screen to come on), and every hour when the battery is below 25% and not charging. When a download fails, a notice and the line "! no data since …" under the clock say why (no internet, the server did not answer, secure connection error, server error…). When Open-Meteo is busy (HTTP 503 "overloaded") or does not answer, the forecast comes from MET Norway (api.met.no, the Norwegian weather institute) and the line under the clock says "· MET Norway"; after a "busy" the app goes straight to MET Norway for 20 minutes. Until new data comes, the last saved weather stays on the screen. Without internet the app does not try again and again: it waits for the network to come back, then brings the weather at once.	Ilm, hoiatused ja maavärinad tulevad ise, ka suletud rakendusega (vidinate jaoks): iga 15 minuti järel, kui ekraan on sees, iga 30 minuti järel, kui ekraan on väljas, üldse mitte päikeseloojangu ja -tõusu vahel, kui ekraan on väljas (ootab, kuni ekraan süttib), ja kord tunnis, kui aku on alla 25% ja ei lae. Kui allalaadimine ebaõnnestub, ütlevad teade ja rida „! andmeid pole alates …“ kella all põhjuse (internetti pole, server ei vastanud, turvalise ühenduse viga, serveri viga…). Kui Open-Meteo on hõivatud (HTTP 503 „overloaded“) või ei vasta, tuleb prognoos MET Norwaylt (api.met.no, Norra meteoroloogiainstituut) ja kella all olev rida ütleb „· MET Norway“; pärast „hõivatud“ läheb rakendus 20 minutiks otse MET Norwayle. Kuni uued andmed tulevad, jääb ekraanile viimati salvestatud ilm. Ilma internetita ei proovi rakendus ikka ja jälle: see ootab, kuni võrk tagasi tuleb, ja toob kohe ilma.
