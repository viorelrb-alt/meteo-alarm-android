No data collected: no account, no ads, no statistics. To get the weather, only the place's coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo (weather, pollen) and, when Open-Meteo is overloaded, to MET Norway (weather); the warnings come from MeteoAlarm, the earthquakes from EMSC (seismicportal.eu). Meteo Alarm widgets	Widgeti Meteo Alarm
Hold a free spot on the home screen, choose Widgets, then Meteo Alarm: 2x2, 4x1, 4x2, 4x2 · days and "any size", each with its preview; it goes on the screen at once, without a settings window. There are three layouts: 2x2 (square: icon, temperature, town, next sunset or sunrise), 4x1 (a low strip, also with the sky) and 4x2 (wide, also with high / low and the next 5 hours), plus a small 2x1 (icon and temperature) for frames too small for them. Hold it and drag its edges: wide (about 4 columns) and one row = 4x1, wide and taller = 4x2 (the next hours, or the next days for the 4x2 · days; 3 rows or more = 4x3, with both), narrow = 2x2, narrow and one row = 2x1. The rows and columns are those of your home screen (on a tablet a row is much taller than on a phone), so the 4x1 strip works there too. It fills the frame's width; when the frame is taller than the layout needs, the widget keeps its size, centred, with the background only behind it (the rest stays see-through). A layout is never shrunk so much that the texts get too small. With active warnings a row "WARNING!" shows three pills: yellow = gray gray yellow, orange = gray orange orange, red = all red; two codes = the worse one, gray, the other; three codes = red orange yellow. Refreshing by itself	Samostalno osvježavanje
The weather, the warnings and the earthquakes come by themselves, also with the app closed (for the widgets): every 15 minutes while the screen is on, every 30 minutes with the screen off, not at all between sunset and sunrise while the screen is off (it waits for the screen to come on), and every hour when the battery is below 25% and not charging. When a download fails, a notice and the line "! no data since …" under the clock say why (no internet, the server did not answer, secure connection error, server error…). When Open-Meteo is busy (HTTP 503 "overloaded") or does not answer, the forecast comes from MET Norway (api.met.no, the Norwegian weather institute) and the line under the clock says "· MET Norway"; after a "busy" the app goes straight to MET Norway for 20 minutes. Until new data comes, the last saved weather stays on the screen. Without internet the app does not try again and again: it waits for the network to come back, then brings the weather at once.	Vrijeme, upozorenja i potresi stižu sami, i dok je aplikacija zatvorena (za widgete): svakih 15 minuta s uključenim zaslonom, svakih 30 minuta s isključenim, nikako između zalaska i izlaska sunca s isključenim zaslonom (čeka se da se zaslon uključi) i svaki sat kad je baterija ispod 25 % i ne puni se. Kad preuzimanje ne uspije, obavijest i red „! nema podataka od …” ispod sata kažu zašto (nema interneta, poslužitelj nije odgovorio, pogreška sigurne veze, pogreška poslužitelja…). Kad je Open-Meteo zauzet (HTTP 503 „overloaded”) ili ne odgovara, prognoza dolazi s MET Norway (api.met.no, norveški meteorološki institut), a ispod sata piše „· MET Norway”; nakon „zauzet” aplikacija 20 minuta ide izravno na MET Norway. Dok ne stignu novi podaci, na zaslonu ostaje zadnje spremljeno vrijeme. Bez interneta aplikacija ne pokušava iznova i iznova: čeka da se mreža vrati, a zatim odmah donosi vrijeme.
