Current version: v.2.047 · 18.11.2026
MAIN SCREEN
- Earthquake map: a tap on the Earthquake box shows a map with the epicentre, your town, the distance and the felt zones; a tap on the map enlarges it, with the value's history. In Romania, France, Italy, the Netherlands, Switzerland and Greece the official values come first from the country's institute (INFP, BCSF, INGV, KNMI, SED, NOA), then from EMSC.
NOTIFICATIONS
- Earthquakes: checked every 10 minutes, also at night with the screen off; a revised magnitude updates the notice, and a tap on it opens the map.
PARCELS
- Edit parcel: the pencil opens the parcel's page: name, crop (a list by the sowing date's season, or Another crop…), sowing date, area; the corners move on the map.
- History and forecast: drag the indicator to a past day; its values show in a box under the chart and after 10 s it goes back to today.
- Operational status: the crop and its sowing date on top; with a crop the verdict also takes drop drying (ΔT) and its disease into account.
- Parcel coordinates: on the edit page the GPS of each point can be typed in, and under it the satellite view shows the parcel.
- Statistics bubbles: a tap on an hour of the spray window, a day of the charts or a soil row opens a bubble with its explanation; the spray window moves by itself.
- Statistics in the main screen: they open under the operational status (in landscape at the left); ‹ closes them and another parcel's tab keeps them.
- Statistics: for each parcel, the Action of the day and 4 tabs: Operational (the spray window hour by hour, drop drying (ΔT), wind, wet leaf), Water & Soil (rain, evaporation, soil at 6 and 18 cm), Season (degree days against last year, the estimated stage) and Risks 72 h (fungal diseases, ground frost, heat, wind and hail); saved as PDF or CSV.
SETTINGS
- Display language: in ☰ English and the country's language; Other languages: the country's other languages, the big EU languages, Romanian and the phone's (in the US only English and Spanish).
- Design improvements and bug fixes.

Version 1.4 (v.1.400 – v.1.520)
FARMER MODE
- Farmer button: the leaf button next to the buttons turns farmer mode off; another town keeps farmer mode.
- Operational status: on a parcel's screen, only when needed, the spraying verdict, its reason and the risks of the next 24 hours.
- Tabs: the town and the parcels in tabs under the name (in landscape at the bottom); a parcel's screen in sections 1–4.
MAIN SCREEN
- Big compass: a tap on a town in the list shows its mark on the compass; towns in the same direction no longer overlap.
- Five-day forecast: days 3–5 move on by themselves every 3 seconds until the first touch.
- Main screen: the page scrolls up and down under the glass at the top, in sections (Next hours, Five-day forecast, Weather details); the buttons and the battery float at the bottom.
NOTIFICATIONS
- Earthquakes: notifications only for earthquakes at most 300 km from your place; turned on or off in ☰.
- Tornadoes (US): the tornado notifications for your town are turned on or off in ☰.
PARCELS
- Bin: a deleted parcel goes to the bin under the GPS button for 15 days and can be restored from there.
- Map views: round buttons under ＋/− switch between satellite and map and show the rain radar of the last 2 hours.
- New parcel: the pin starts in the middle of the map in sight; the GPS button moves it where you are.
- Parcel corners: in the satellite view the 4 corners are found from the picture and move with a finger; a tap on the card zooms the map on the parcel and shows the corners, Statistics opens its statistics.
- Parcel points: + Point adds up to 3 more points (at most 7), Circle puts them evenly on a circle, a long press on a point deletes it.
- Parcels page: the parcels' cards (name, coordinates, pencil, Statistics · PDF, bell, bin) float over the map, in a row at the bottom or a column on the right; ＋, also the ＋ tab, opens a new parcel's box.
- Pins on the map: a tap on a pin zooms the map on the parcel and shows its name; a second tap shows its points.
SETTINGS
- Display language: buttons in ☰, 2 on a row: English and the country's languages (in the US English and Spanish); in Romania “Other languages (minorities)” shows the rest.
- Icon theme: 6 weather icon themes (Classic, Glossy, Flat, Realistic, Atmospheric, Outline), chosen with coloured dots in ☰; in the app and on the widgets.
- Settings: the ☰ menu is now a window with sections, switches, the colour themes and the System & support bar; refresh by pulling the screen down or tapping “updated”.
UPDATES
- Updates: the app no longer looks for updates by itself, only when you tap ☰ › Check for updates; the new version downloads in the browser and you install it from the downloaded file.
- Design improvements and bug fixes.

Version 1.3 (v.1.300 – v.1.352)
MAIN SCREEN
- Big compass: a tap on the Compass box shows the compass, the GPS coordinates and the 3 nearest towns in colours; Share sends the coordinates.
- Big compass: the town you are in comes first (grey); Share also sends a picture of the window, the nearby towns with distances, the battery, the temperatures (air, ground, at the phone's height) and the wind.
- Boxes: a tap on any box shows its value on top and the explanations under it.
- Export / Import: moves your towns, favourites, parcels and settings to another device through a file (Save, Share; Import with Add or Replace).
- Location: in another country or US state, the small localities are downloaded once, the first time you are there with internet, for the compass and the list of places.
- Location: the villages you pass through join their county's list (for 7 days, or for good if you pass there often); the compass shows the villages too.
- Warnings: a tap on a code or information box shows the forecast values on top (gusts, amounts, temperatures), then the interval and the explanations.
- Weather icons: in the theme's colours; all of them move for 3 seconds, also after every refresh and on the widgets.
WIDGETS
- Widget 4x2 · list: a big temperature and the next 4 days (one icon each) or hours, chosen in its settings; the town after the compass's town mark.
- Widgets: the coloured code lines on every widget, without the WARNING text; without codes, one blue line with the first information still in force (e.g. Frost).
