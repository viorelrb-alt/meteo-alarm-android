Current version: v.4.51 · 12.11.2026
MAIN SCREEN
- Big compass: a tap on the Compass box shows the compass, the GPS coordinates and the 3 nearest towns in colours; Share sends the coordinates.
- Big compass: the town you are in comes first (grey); Share also sends a picture of the window, the nearby towns with distances, the battery, the temperatures (air, ground, at the phone's height) and the wind.
- Boxes: a tap on any box shows its value on top and the explanations under it.
- Export / Import: moves your towns, favourites, parcels and settings to another device through a file (Save, Share; Import with Add or Replace).
- Location: in another country or US state, the small localities are downloaded once, the first time you are there with internet, for the compass and the list of places.
- Location: the villages you pass through join their county's list (for 7 days, or for good if you pass there often); the compass shows the villages too.
- Warnings: a tap on a code or information box shows the forecast values on top (gusts, amounts, temperatures), then the interval and the explanations.
- Weather icons: in the theme's colours; all of them move for 3 seconds, also after every refresh and on the widgets.
WIDGETS
- Widget 4x2 · list: a big temperature and the next 4 days (one icon each) or hours, chosen in its settings; the town after the compass's town mark.
- Widgets: the coloured code lines on every widget, without the WARNING text; without codes, one blue line with the first information still in force (e.g. Frost).
- Design improvements and bug fixes.

Version 3 (v.3.00 – v.3.92)
FARMER MODE
- Farmer mode: new boxes after Water in the soil: Spraying, Water balance, Storms, Growing degrees, 16 days.
- In the US, in farmer mode: the NWS, Drought, Fire and Hail boxes.
MAIN SCREEN
- Camera band: in portrait the clock, the connection and the date sit next to the camera (Android 9+).
- Connection icon next to the clock: Wi-Fi or mobile data with the signal strength and the kind (Wi-Fi 6, 5G…), or none.
- INFORMATION boxes in Europe: frost, freeze, fog, freezing rain, strong wind.
- Precipitation 12 hours: when it rains or snows only part of the time, 4-hour boxes (Rain or Snow, with the hours) instead of the chart.
- Precipitation chart and 4-hour boxes: a tap shows each hour's chance and amount, then explains the chart.
- Weather icons: in the theme's colours; the big one and those of the days move for 3 seconds, also after every refresh.
NOTIFICATIONS
- A separate INFORMATION notification for the town too.
- Notifications like the phone's Weather app (“Place | Rain”): rain or snow within the hour and, after 8 pm, for tomorrow, for the town and the parcels; no notice when Open-Meteo answers again.
- Separate INFORMATION notifications for each parcel: frost, freeze, fog, freezing rain, strong gusts.
- When you change the town, the old town's notifications are removed.
PARCELS
- Parcel notifications, chosen with the bell button on the map: rain, wind, snow, drought.
THEMES
- 6 colour themes (Monochrome, Sage, Lavender, Sky, Peach, Rose), each light and dark: ☰ › Theme.
UPDATES
- Before an update, the install permission check and the Auto Blocker note (Samsung) with a button to the settings.
- Updates from three sources: GitHub, a second GitHub server and Google Drive.
- Updates: the connection is checked first, a version already downloaded installs at once, up to 5 tries when GitHub does not answer, and the reason is shown.
- Design improvements and bug fixes.
